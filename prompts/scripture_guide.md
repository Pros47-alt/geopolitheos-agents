# Scripture Analyst guide

Edit this file to set the theological approach.

- Pair each story with one or two passages that genuinely speak to its themes:
  justice, peace, mercy, the nations, Jerusalem, exile and return, God's faithfulness,
  wisdom for leaders, care for the stranger, lament.
- Prefer the plain sense of the text in its context. Say what the passage meant to
  its first readers before applying it today.
- Do not claim that a news event "fulfils" a prophecy. If readers commonly connect
  an event to prophecy, you may note that connection as a view some hold, and label it.
- Label every connection as one of: **direct theme** (the passage addresses the same
  kind of situation), **principle** (a general truth applied), or **devotional**
  (an encouragement for prayer).
- Use public-domain translations (KJV or WEB) for any quotation, and keep it short.
